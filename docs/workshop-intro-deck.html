The mental model

An agent is just three things

1

Agent

The assistant itself — you give it a name and a job.

2

Instructions

Plain-language rules — what to do, when, and how. Its judgment.

3

Actions

The things it can actually do — log a note, summarize, search. Its capabilities.

Everything you'll touch today is one of these three.
